Human–Plant–Computer Interaction Workshop

What if plants weren't resources, but participants?

A full-day, hands-on workshop imagining and building future systems where plants, people and technology shape one another — through speculative design, plant sensing, VR and physical prototyping. No experience needed.

The day

From imagining futures to making them tangible.

Morning

Imagining plant futures

Meet living plants and take on a speculative scenario set in 2050 — how might human–plant–technology relationships respond to an ecological, social or community challenge?

  • Observe & interact with living plants
  • Sketch imagined systems in VR (Open Brush)
  • Backcast toward a possible future
Afternoon

Making plant interfaces

Speculation becomes experiment. Using pre-configured kits and a no-code tutorial, read plant and environmental data, then build a physical prototype of your idea.

  • Read plant & environment data, no code
  • Prototype with clay, cardboard, LEGO, sensors
  • Present & discuss the futures that emerged
